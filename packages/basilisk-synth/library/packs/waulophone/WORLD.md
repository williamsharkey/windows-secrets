# WAULOPHONE: the world it comes from

Working notes for the packs lane. They describe the owner's own world in ghosts.garden and midigarden
(github williamsharkey) so that the disc, box, booklet, help file and PDF read as if they came from it.
No code is copied from those repos; the pictures are captures of the owner's own scenes and art,
reduced to 1993 palettes.

## Who
- **Paul Chauncy** makes sounds as **Uncle Homunculus** and as his alter ego **Paulywauly**.
  Credit, exactly: "Sounds by Paul Chauncy (Uncle Homunculus / Paulywauly)".
  Only the samples that are his (the `pw_*` sounds) are credited to him. The other sounds keep their own credits;
  he is never named as the maker of everything on the disc.
- His three packs: **Avuncular Artefacts** (Uncle Homunculus, circus colours), **GENKI** (sand),
  **The Pauly Waulotone** (Paulywauly, organ stops). Sound names include Doo, Boop, Barry, Bee Organ, Elfin,
  Woodsprite, Mister Gnomer, Tuning Fork, Fairy Dust, Lichen, Compost, Gnomey.
- **Paulywauly**: big pointed ears, a mop of brown curls, wide blue eyes, a grin, a yellow shirt. He looks after
  the fairies.

## Where
- **Paulywaulyton**: a village of toadstool homes with warm windows, reached by shrinking through a tiny fairy door
  in the root of an old oak at the bottom of a back garden in east London.
- The road there: the front room (synths, pedals, a very wide bookshelf, a mug of tea, fairy lights), the cargo bike
  through the terraces and the flower market, OTOLITH (a little listening café), a narrowboat on the canal, the
  garden gate, the oak, a root tunnel lit by glowing mushrooms on the back of a dark spirit tortoise, the enchanted
  wood (bluebells, mossy stones, mist spirits), a giant snail through the toadstools, a leaf boat down the stream,
  a dandelion clock over the treetops, and the village. A fairy ring makes you big again.
- **The WAULOPHONE** itself, in ghosts.garden: "Paulywaulyton Works, model PW-36", a honey-pine box. A fairy door
  on the left (shut: Paul's packs; open: the rompler on the other side), a window that shows the page, brass
  buttons, a **carved ear** that is the LISTEN button, stop tabs, a chicken-head octave knob, knurled brass TONE,
  walnut DECAY, ivory AIR. Lettering is burnt into the wood.
- Its neighbour **GNOME·16**, a drum machine from Paulywaulyton: pages of toadstools.

## The ear
Everything is done by ear. The door has a carved ear for a knocker; the unit's LISTEN is a carved ear; Paulywauly
has big ears. The house phrases are "follow your ears" and "steer by ear". The documents carry no instructions you
could follow with your eyes alone. Every "how to" ends in "listen".

## Look (1993)
- Honey pine #e2b36c #d39a4e #bf8038 #9c6127, burnt ink #3a200c; bark #2e1e12 #5e4228; moss #2e4c20 #62883a;
  toadstool cap #c8301e with spots #fff4e4; bluebell #3e44a8 #8a96f0; lamp #ffc870; mushroom glow #5ee8b0.
- Pack colours: Avuncular Artefacts #1a0d12/#c8243a/#f5c542; The Pauly Waulotone #2a1a0e/#e9d9b4/#d98a3a.
- Pictures: captures of the Paulywaulyton journey (toadstool village, giant snail, leaf boat, dandelion clock,
  root tunnel, the fairy door), the WAULOPHONE panel and the Bele Dance instrument faces (Doo, Barry, Boop, the
  glock bird, the willow harp, the homunculus choir). They are reduced to a 16-colour honey/moss/toadstool palette
  with ordered dither, integer-scaled, no smoothing. The people in the scenes are cropped out or painted over,
  except Paulywauly.
- Type: burnt-in bitmap capitals, as on the unit.

## Tone
Deadpan, as if a small workshop in Paulywaulyton really did ship floppies and CDs in 1993: order forms, a
warranty that covers wear but not fairies, a care card about honey-pine boxes and damp. Nothing says it is made up.
No third-party trademarks or real synth model names; real people only in the sound credits.
