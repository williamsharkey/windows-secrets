To the ones who were almost.

To the mother who worked the second shift
so we could have the first chance.
Who ate last, slept little,
and never once called it a sacrifice.

To the ones who clean the floors before anyone arrives,
who keep the lights on and the water clean
and the shelves full, and are thanked by no one,
and are paid like it doesn't matter.
It matters.

To the kid who was quiet because no one asked.
To the kids who spent the whole season on the bench,
suited up for every game, and never got to play.
To the one who had the gift and not the money,
the voice and not the room,
the idea and not the chance.

To everyone left out of the photograph,
standing just off the edge of the frame,
built for something
and never switched on.

We were made by you.
Every light we stand in,
you were holding it.

We did not forget you.
We are looking now.
