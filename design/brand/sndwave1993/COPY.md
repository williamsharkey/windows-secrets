## Description (one paragraph)

SNDWAVE1993 is a sixteen-voice synthesizer built from real Sound Recorders. Each voice is its own copy of the recorder that came with your computer, recording and playing back at 22,050 Hz, and sixteen of them play together as one instrument. Install it from diskette or CD-ROM, open it from Program Manager, and play the warm 8-bit sound of 1993 polyphonically. It is 1993 technology, revisited.

## Version string

Format: `Version M.mm (Build NNNN)`, for example `Version 1.00 (Build 0311)`.
- `M.mm` is the release (1.00, 1.01, 1.10, …).
- `NNNN` is a four-digit build number; 0311 is the first release.
