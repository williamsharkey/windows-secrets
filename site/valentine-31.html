Valentine 3.11

A valentine, made by Windows for Workgroups 3.11

Type a few words. A real Windows 3.11 machine, running right now, types them onto a Cardfile card, deals Solitaire's hearts into a heart around it, lets the Clock keep the time, and sends back the picture.

Nothing is redrawn to look old: the card, the cards and the clock are the 1993 programs themselves, photographed straight off the machine's screen.

How it's made
  1. Your words wait in line, then go down a serial cable to Windows.
  2. A small 16-bit program opens Cardfile's Index dialog and types the name, then types the message onto the card, key by key.
  3. It deals Ace to King of hearts from Solitaire's own card library along a heart.
  4. Everything repaints, and the screen is photographed.

Your card is private: it has a random address only you get, and it's deleted after 24 hours. Kind words only, please.
