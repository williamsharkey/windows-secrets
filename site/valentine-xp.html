Write a few words for someone. A real Windows XP machine lifts Calculator's keys into a heart, sets your letter in Notepad, picks red in Paint, and sends you back the picture.

Save the picture

"Save as a page" gives you one .html file with the picture inside it: it opens by itself in any browser, offline, forever. The picture is kept on our side for 24 hours, reachable only through your link, then deleted. There is no gallery.

What happens to your words

  • The machine is Windows XP, really running, in a virtual machine of its own. Each valentine starts from the same quiet desktop.
  • Calculator opens in Scientific view, out of sight. Its push keys are taken out of Calculator with SetParent and stood along a heart curve, in mirror pairs. They are still Calculator's keys, painted by Calculator.
  • Notepad opens with its font set to Times New Roman italic, and your letter goes in as Notepad's own text. Its title becomes the name you wrote.
  • When the letter leaves room, Paint's colour box joins it, with red already picked.
  • Nothing is moved while it's on screen, so nothing smears. Every part is placed while hidden, then shown all at once. The whole screen is repainted and left to settle before the picture is taken.
  • Letters keep to what XP's fonts can draw: Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, and a ♥. Heart emoji become ♥, and other emoji are left out.
