SNDWAVE1993 Ensemble - CITYPOP1993

One Windows 3.11 plays the whole album. Nothing is downloaded until Play.

Notepad - ENSEMBLE.TXT

CITYPOP1993, all twelve tracks and every part in them, played live in this browser by one SNDWAVE1993 Ensemble: one emulated Windows for Workgroups 3.11, up to sixteen lanes, each lane its own preset on its own Sound Recorders. Every note is a real SOUNDREC.EXE.

Between parts, a lane changes preset. Windows builds the next sound with Sound Recorder's own menus while the lane is silent, one recorder at a time, and the strip shows it; a sound built once is kept on R: and the next lane that needs it just opens the file. Each part gets as many recorders as it ever plays at once, and a lane with nothing to play for a while gives its recorders back. Where a part starts before its build is done, its first notes come late. The counts are below the screen while it plays.

No echo or reverb: the Ensemble mixes level and pan only.

The first track comes already built: Windows is frozen with its sixteen strips and the first track's sounds on their recorders, so it starts a few seconds after Play. About 55 MB is downloaded in all, most of it the Rainy Loft disc, a piece at a time ahead of each track. It wants a recent desktop browser.
