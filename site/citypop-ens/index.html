SNDWAVE2000 Ensemble - CITYPOP DEPROGRAMMED

One Windows 3.11 plays the whole album. Nothing is downloaded until Play.

Notepad - ENSEMBLE.TXT

CITYPOP DEPROGRAMMED, all twelve tracks, played live in this browser by one SNDWAVE2000 Ensemble: one emulated Windows for Workgroups 3.11, ten lanes, each lane its own preset on its own Sound Recorders. Every note is a real SOUNDREC.EXE.

Between parts, a lane changes preset. Windows builds the next sound with Sound Recorder's own menus while the lane is silent, one recorder at a time, and the strip shows it. Where a part starts before its build is done, its first notes come late. The counts are below the screen while it plays.

Ten lanes, not sixteen: the quietest ambience and the doubled parts of the bigger tracks are left out. No echo or reverb: the Ensemble mixes level and pan only.

About 50 MB is downloaded, most of it the Rainy Loft disc, a piece at a time ahead of each track. It wants a recent desktop browser, and about twenty seconds to start: Windows builds the first track's ten sounds before the first note.
