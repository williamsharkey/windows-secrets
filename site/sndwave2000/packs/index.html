SNDWAVE2000 · Sound Pack Explorer

Sound packs

Twenty discs of presets for SNDWAVE2000, 1,184 sounds in all, each disc from a small company that never existed, with its box, its manual and its Windows help file. Open a pack to hear every preset and read its recipe: the DNA in Sound Recorder's own words. The owner went through all of them; ★ marks a favourite, and a number is the owner's rating out of 10.

20 packs, 1,184 presets. The previews for 1.3.0 are being rendered on the release build; until then the owner's top picks play the demos already made.
